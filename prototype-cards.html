Kitob kartochkasi

Baseline is the redesigned card you just shipped. B pushes it editorial (title on cover); C is a dense horizontal row for search/list views.

A · Current — designed cover, glass heart, title/author, price + teal cart button. Clean, grid-friendly.
B · Editorial overlay — title & author sit on a scrim over the cover, rating chip top-left, slim buy bar below. More visual impact, less white space; title legibility depends on cover art.
C · Horizontal compact — cover left, details right, with room for meta (pages · year). Great density for search results & wishlist; weaker for a showcase grid.

Nashriyot kartochkasi

Baseline is the current logo-tile. B adds a branded gradient header + a peek of the publisher's covers. C is an info-dense row (city · founded · count).

A · Current logo tile — logo on cream, name + book count. Simple, but the cream tile can feel flat and doesn't use the publisher's brand color.
B · Branded header + cover peek — publisher's brand color as a gradient header, three spine covers peeking. More alive, previews the catalogue; a bit taller.
C · Detail row — logo + name + (city · founded) + prominent count. Best for the full publishers list where scanning & info matter more than showcase.
